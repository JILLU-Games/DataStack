# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers of **DataStack** and the **[Vib Tools](https://vib.tools/)** open-source community pledge to make participation in our project and community a harassment-free, constructive, and welcoming experience for everyone, regardless of background, experience level, gender identity, disability, ethnicity, religion, or nationality.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive, productive environment include:

- Demonstrating empathy, respect, and constructive communication toward other contributors and users.
- Giving and gracefully accepting technical feedback focused on code quality, maintainability, and clarity.
- Focusing on practical solutions that benefit users and the broader open-source community.
- Respecting repository architecture boundaries, documentation standards, and security policies.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, personal attacks, trolling, insulting, or derogatory comments.
- Publishing others' private information (such as physical or electronic addresses) without explicit permission.
- Submitting malicious code, spam, or intentionally disruptive pull requests/issues.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional technical community.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers at **Vib Tools** are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces (GitHub repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, and official communication channels) and also applies when an individual is officially representing the project or community in public spaces.

## Enforcement & Contact

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the **Vib Tools** team at:

- **Support & Moderation Email**: [support@vibtools.io](mailto:support@vibtools.io)
- **General Inquiries**: [hello@vibtools.io](mailto:hello@vibtools.io)
- **Website**: [https://vib.tools/](https://vib.tools/)

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
